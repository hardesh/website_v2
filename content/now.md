## Beliefs

### Robotics and AI will be good for humanity

I believe robotics and AI will largely benefit humanity. Some jobs will be replaced, and new jobs will be created in their place.

### We will become an interplanetary species

Humanity is on its way to becoming an interplanetary species, and that day gets closer with every successful Starship launch and every ambitious company that enters the field. I think we’ll get there in the next 20 to 30 years.

### Work that improves lives

I love working on products that improve the lives of people across the world, and working with people who see the good in others and in humanity.
